[B]Update - v6.8.005 - Nov 12th, 2026[/B]
--[Fix] MDBList Finish Watching no longer fills up with episodes and movies you finished. Umbrella now closes the MDBList playback session when an item is completed, and resuming past 80% no longer marks it watched early.
--[Feature] Added MDBList Tools > Clean up finished items in MDBList Finish Watching (also in Settings > Shortcuts). It removes items you already finished that older versions left in Finish Watching, and keeps unfinished items and rewatches in progress.
